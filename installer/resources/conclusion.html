VibeCheck is installed.

Open it from Applications or Spotlight. On first launch it scans your plugins, which can take a minute for a large library. Every launch after that only looks at what is new.

  • Command+1 to Command+5 switch between pages.
  • In Graph Analyzer, hover any graph for exact values.
  • Manual, at the bottom of the sidebar, explains how to read every result.

If you chose the command line tool, try vibecheck --selftest in Terminal.
