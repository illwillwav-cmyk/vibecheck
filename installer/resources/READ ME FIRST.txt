VibeCheck: how to open it the first time
========================================

This build is not signed with an Apple developer certificate, so macOS asks you to
approve it once. That is normal for an app from a friend rather than the App Store.
Nothing is wrong with the download.

If you used the disk image (.dmg)
  1. Drag VibeCheck onto Applications.
  2. Open Applications, then right-click (or Control-click) VibeCheck and choose Open.
  3. In the box that appears, click Open.
  After that it opens normally every time.

If macOS still refuses ("VibeCheck cannot be opened")
  1. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
  2. Scroll down to the message about VibeCheck and click Open Anyway.
  3. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

If you used the installer (.pkg)
  Double-click it. If macOS says it cannot be opened, go to System Settings,
  Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway next to the installer's name.

If none of that works, paste this into Terminal once, then open the app:
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VibeCheck.app

What it does
  VibeCheck lists your audio plugins, measures what they do to sound, runs
  robustness checks, and reads each plugin file for signs of how it was made.
  It never connects to the internet and never sends anything anywhere.

To remove it
  Drag VibeCheck to the Trash, or run "Uninstall VibeCheck.command" from the
  disk image to also remove its settings and the installer receipt.
