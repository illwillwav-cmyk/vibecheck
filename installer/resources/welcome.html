What's the vibe?

VibeCheck measures what an audio plugin does to your signal, checks that it behaves, and compares it with others.

  • Graph Analyzer runs test signals through a plugin offline and reports distortion, frequency and phase response, dynamics, latency and noise.
  • Plugin Health runs thirteen robustness checks: valid output, any buffer size, honest latency, saved settings.
  • A/B Compare and Performance Profiler put two plugins side by side and tell you how many instances a session can hold.

This installer puts VibeCheck in your Applications folder. It takes a few seconds and needs less than 20 MB.

VibeCheck checks once at launch whether a newer version exists, by reading one small public file. It sends nothing about you or your plugins.
