What's the vibe?

VibeCheck measures what an audio plugin does to your signal, and reads its binary for signs of how it was made.

  • Graph Analyzer runs test signals through a plugin offline and reports distortion, frequency and phase response, dynamics, latency and noise.
  • AI Check reads each plugin file on disk and shows the evidence behind a score. It never loads or runs the plugin.
  • A/B Compare and Performance Profiler put two plugins side by side and tell you how many instances a session can hold.

This installer puts VibeCheck in your Applications folder. It takes a few seconds and needs less than 20 MB.

VibeCheck does not connect to the internet and does not send anything anywhere.
